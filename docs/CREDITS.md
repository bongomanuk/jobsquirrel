# Credits

## Cascade

The dashboard's reactive surfaces use **Cascade**, a reactive front-end
framework by **Robert Renbris** ([erobwen/liquefy](https://github.com/erobwen/liquefy)).

- The Activity run roster, the grid rows and the role drawer are Cascade islands.
- The browser bundle is vendored at `tools/web/vendor/cascade.js` and built from
  `@liquefy/cascade.reactive`, `@liquefy/cascade.component` and
  `@liquefy/cascade.dom` 2.x.
- Cascade is licensed under the ISC License, copyright (c) 2026 Robert Renbris.
  The full notice and the build entry are in
  `tools/web/vendor/cascade.NOTICE`.

Cascade's own documentation and demos are at
[erobwen.github.io/liquefy/cascade](https://erobwen.github.io/liquefy/cascade).
